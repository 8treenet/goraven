## Description: <br>
Create, inspect, and edit Microsoft Excel workbooks and XLSX files with reliable formulas, dates, types, formatting, recalculation, and template preservation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers, analysts, and agents use this skill when spreadsheet deliverables need dependable workbook inspection or edits, especially where formulas, dates, formatting, merged cells, workbook structure, template preservation, or cross-platform behavior matter. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Workbook reads or edits can damage formulas, formatting, dates, identifiers, macros, or template structure when applied without review. <br>
Mitigation: Keep backups of important spreadsheets and review modified workbooks, especially formula-heavy or business-critical files, before relying on them. <br>
Risk: Formula caches, copied references, hidden sheets, named ranges, and cross-application differences can make a workbook appear correct while preserving stale or incorrect logic. <br>
Mitigation: Recalculate and inspect representative formulas, ranges, hidden workbook structure, and visual layout before delivery. <br>
